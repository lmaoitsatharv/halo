Good to know

Which music players work with Halo?+

Automatic integration currently supports the Spotify desktop app on Windows. Spotify Web and other music players aren’t currently supported. Halo doesn’t require a Spotify login or API key.

How are the lyrics synchronized?+

Halo gets line-synced lyrics from LRCLIB, with manual timing adjustment and local .lrc imports. It estimates word timing between line timestamps; exact word timestamps aren’t currently supported. Spotify’s public API does not provide Halo’s lyrics.

Does Music sync detect the beat?+

Music sync responds to system output loudness, including sound from other apps on the output device. It doesn’t detect exact beats or precise tempo.

What happens to imported lyrics?+

Local .lrc imports apply to the current song and reset when the song changes. Keeping imports associated with songs across playback sessions and app restarts is on the roadmap.

Can I try the visuals without Spotify?+

Halo includes a visual demo with original timed text. It previews the lighting and lyric styles without playing audio. The interactive visuals on this page are concept illustrations, not screenshots of the Windows app.

Can I keep the movement gentle?+

Halo supports reduced motion, as well as slow Idle and stationary Still lighting modes. This page also respects your device’s reduced-motion preference and includes a motion pause control above.

Planned features. Not available today.
